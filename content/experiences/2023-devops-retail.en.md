Industrialisation engagement for a retail group whose e-commerce platform suffered from manual, risky deployments.

- Migrated 12 services to Kubernetes (EKS) with Terraform, with zero downtime.
- Went from one deployment per week to several per day thanks to GitLab CI pipelines.
- Cut build times by 4x and introduced ephemeral staging environments.
- Trained internal teams: Docker/K8s workshops and operations documentation.
