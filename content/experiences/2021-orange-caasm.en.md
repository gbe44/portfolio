Four years as technical lead of a Cyber Asset Attack Surface Management tool: mapping every exposed asset, cross-referencing inventory sources and orchestrating vulnerability scanners to give the VOC a consolidated view of the attack surface.

Working closely with the VOC manager, from requirements gathering to demonstrations, I designed and grew a microservices architecture (10+ containers, 3 databases) with a Python backend (Flask, FastAPI, Celery) and a React frontend: a centralized inventory of 150,000+ assets fed by 5 third-party APIs, asynchronous collection and event-driven scanners.

Outcome: about 70 days per year saved for the main user, 50+ automated scans per week and a significant reduction in vulnerabilities.
