Management simulation of a **road haulage company** across Europe: truck fleet, driver hiring and morale, contracts and commodity trading, maintenance, finances, company progression, races and mini-games.

- Cross-platform **Flutter** app (Android, Windows), Riverpod state management, local **SQLite** database through Drift, push notifications, accelerated in-game time engine.
- About thirty gameplay services: contract generation, mission incidents, driver regulations, AI manager, quests, prestige, casual and hardcore modes.
- **LLM** layer in preparation: a chat system with the drivers to create interaction and trigger dynamically generated **in-game events**.
