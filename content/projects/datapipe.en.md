Datapipe was born out of a frustration on a client project: mainstream data orchestrators are oversized for 90% of real needs. Datapipe lets you describe a pipeline in YAML and run it locally or in CI.

- ~400 stars on GitHub, a dozen contributors.
- Used in production by two former clients.
- Zero dependencies outside the Python standard library.
